01 / 13 — Investment memorandum

Investment thesis

Layerlab occupies the high-value middle ground between ad-hoc 3D print shops and tooling-heavy injection molding: rapid iterations, low minimum orders, consistent quality and scalable SKU economics.

Structural advantage

Low MOQ × fast iteration

Digitally controlled additive manufacturing allows small, custom batches without expensive production molds.

Execution thesis

One factory. Three distribution channels.

Shared printers and QA systems create cross-category operating leverage as product-market fit is proven.

07A — Legal readiness & investor protection

Build the business on a legal foundation

Proposed setup for an Indonesian-owned 3D-printing micro-factory in Depok. These are pre-investment action items, not claims that registrations or agreements have already been completed.

01 · Incorporate a regular PT

Notarial deed, AHU legal-entity approval, articles of association and documented share capital. A multi-shareholder PT is more suitable for external equity investors than a sole-owner PT perorangan.

BEFORE INVESTMENT

02 · Obtain OSS licensing

Register the PT for NIB and verify applicable KBLI 2025 codes, including 3D printing activity (18113), retail and any electronics assembly. Confirm OSS risk classification, standard certificates or additional permits, premises zoning and environmental obligations.

BEFORE OPERATIONS

03 · Protect investor rights

Use a negotiated share subscription agreement and shareholders' agreement: equity issuance, voting, information rights, reserved matters, dilution, transfer/exit rights, founder vesting if appropriate, dispute resolution and related-party transaction controls.

BEFORE FUNDING

04 · Separate company finances

Corporate bank account, company tax registration, proper bookkeeping and monthly investor reporting. Transfer funds to the PT under signed documents, not to a founder's personal account.

AT CLOSING

05 · Manufacturing compliance

Check Depok premises use, fire and electrical safety, ABS/ASA ventilation, resin waste handling, worker safety, and product-specific requirements. Motorcycle parts must be tested and should not be marketed as safety-certified without evidence.

BEFORE PRODUCTION

06 · Protect designs and brand

Check Layerlab trademark availability; document ownership of CAD files, firmware, customer designs and employee/contractor IP. Obtain permission for third-party fan imagery, logos and licensed character designs.

Each order card stores ID, SKU, variant, quantity, material, printer, deadline and status. WIP limit: eight simultaneously active printer jobs. A failed print creates a tracked reprint ticket.

Factory layout, inventory & shipping SOP

Two-bin material Kanban

One active supply bin plus one reserve bin per priority material/color. Opening the reserve triggers replenishment, sized to measured consumption and supplier lead time.

Stock-first scheduling

An order for 10 mounts with 3 approved finished units in stock schedules only 7 new prints. Approved stock and in-progress inventory are tracked separately.
